# ========================================================================
# Nine Ruthenian branches of the Commonwealth, 1588-1772.
# Authenticity: A for the dated acts, B for their consequences - the options are the
# mod's own, and the widow of the Volhynian prince is a composite of several documented
# Orthodox foundations, not one person.
# Sources:
#   1620: the Patriarch of Jerusalem consecrates a metropolitan of Kyiv and bishops; the
#   Crown declares them illegal. 1632: the Articles of Pacification for the Greek
#   religion at Wladyslaw IV's coronation diet recognise the Orthodox hierarchy; the
#   Kyiv Brotherhood school and the Lavra school merge into the Kyiv Collegium.
#   1615: Halshka Hulevychivna gives her Kyiv estate to the Epiphany Brotherhood.
#   The Left Bank colonised by magnates in the 1620s-40s under free-year charters.
#   1588: the Third Statute of Lithuania in chancery Ruthenian; 1696: the diet replaces
#   Ruthenian with Polish in the Grand Duchy's chancery.
#   1672: Kamianets falls to the Ottomans and the treaty of Buchach cedes Podolia; the
#   diet refuses it and Sobieski wins at Khotyn (1673); Karlowitz returns Podolia (1699).
#   1667 Andrusovo and 1686 Eternal Peace: Kyiv passes to Muscovy for good.
#   1767-68: the Repnin diet and the Dissident question; Confederation of Bar (1768-72).
# Reachability: the dated events (plc_hierarchy.1-2, plc_zadnieprze.1, plc_chancery.1-2,
# plc_princes.1 and .3, plc_flight.1, plc_buchach.1 and .3, plc_bar.1, plc_patrons.1-3) are MTTH
# events; the others are called by options, the decisions or the disaster. The Confederation of
# Bar disaster is gated on the plc_bar.1 flags, so a Crown that never faces the Dissident
# question never meets it.
# ========================================================================
